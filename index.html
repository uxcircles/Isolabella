Benvenuti

Traditional dishes from Italy, with a Mediterranean touch

Everything is cooked with care and served with attention to taste and presentation: a long menu of pasta, risotto, pizza and salads, Italian-style seafood, and rustic meat dishes that are not to be missed.

Our team look after you the Italian way — warm hospitality, a welcoming room and wines to match every plate.
